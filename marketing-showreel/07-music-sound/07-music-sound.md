# Stage 8 — Music & Sound Design

No copyrighted commercial tracks referenced — this is a style brief for either licensing a stock track matching this description or commissioning/generating an original one.

## Overall mood
Premium technology/product commercial — restrained, confident, a little cinematic tension building toward a clean release at the CTA. Think "Apple keynote product film" energy more than "hype trailer." Warm-neutral tonal palette (matches the visual grade): avoid icy/glassy synth patches, favor warmer analog-leaning pads and felt-piano-adjacent textures with a light electronic pulse layered underneath.

## Structure (mapped to the 40s cut)

| Section | Time | BPM feel | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 0:00–0:05.5 | ~78–86 (felt, not strictly on-grid yet) | Low sub-bass drone + one slow riser starting almost inaudibly. No percussion yet — establishes scale and quiet confidence. |
| Build 1 (Paynora block) | 0:05.5–0:19 | ramps to ~96–104 | A soft rhythmic pulse enters at the first "Proof" label (scene 3). Percussive ticks sync to UI reveals (scene 4–5). A bright, confident chime hits exactly on the Action Center push-in (scene 6) — the emotional high point of this half. |
| Turn (Proof #2 label) | 0:22–0:23.5 | brief drop | Short percussive impact + a half-second near-silence before Nexora block starts — a deliberate breath, not a full stop. |
| Build 2 (Nexora block) | 0:23.5–0:32.5 | ~100–112 | Tone warms slightly (matches the bronze/violet grade). A soft "message received" chime on scene 9, rhythmic ticks per report-card reveal on scene 10, one bright accent hit on the mobile-orbit shot (scene 11). |
| Final build | 0:32.5–0:38.5 | ~112–120 | Tightest, most driving section — rhythmic hits synced to each tech-stack label (scene 12) and each service bullet locking in (scene 13). This is the only section that should feel close to "hype." |
| CTA hit | 0:38.5–0:40 | resolves | One clean low bass/chime hit exactly as the CTA frame appears, then the track resolves and fades to near-silence — leave the last ~0.3s clean so the Telegram handle reads without a busy mix underneath. |

## Specific SFX cues
- **Scene 4 (landing):** soft glassy UI-chime as the floating stat card catches the light sweep.
- **Scene 6 (Action Center):** a single confident "confirm" click sound timed to the camera settling on "Approve" — this is the ad's key beat, give it the cleanest, most premium-sounding hit in the whole mix.
- **Scene 9 (AI chat):** a soft, warm "message received" tone — not a generic notification ding, something closer to a soft mallet/bell.
- **Transitions (scenes 3, 8):** one percussive impact each, the second slightly warmer/lower in timbre than the first to mark the tonal shift from Paynora (cool) to Nexora (warm).
- **Scene 12/13 (stack + services):** short, tight rhythmic ticks — think a muted mallet or a processed UI-click — one per label/bullet, building tempo slightly toward the CTA.
- **CTA:** single low, warm bass hit + a very short reverb tail. No cymbal crash, no "epic" trailer boom — this should feel like a product settling into place, not an explosion.

## What to avoid
- Trap hi-hats / dubstep wobble bass — reads as generic and undercuts "premium."
- Generic "corporate stock music" ukulele/whistling — wrong register entirely for this positioning.
- Any vocal sample or hook — keep it instrumental so it never competes with the on-screen text.
