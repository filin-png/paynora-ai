# Stage 6 — AI Video Generation Prompts

General rule applied throughout: whenever a real Paynora screenshot is the source image, the prompt explicitly instructs the model to preserve the UI pixel-for-pixel and animate the camera/environment around it, never to redesign or regenerate the interface. Nexora scenes have no source image (see 03-screenshots/README.md) and are text-to-video, generated as clearly stylized recreations of the product's own documented design tokens — not passed off as real UI.

## Why two different generators

- **Kling 2.5** is used wherever the shot needs a *precise, controlled camera move* (a specific push-in that lands exactly on a button, an orbital move around a 3D object, or a from-scratch environment with staged sequential reveals) — it's the more reliable of the two for camera choreography and material rendering from a pure text prompt.
- **PixVerse** is used for the real Paynora screenshots because, in practice, it tends to preserve fine UI text/detail slightly better under moderate camera motion (dolly/pan) than heavier stylization-prone models — important since these are real product screenshots that must stay legible and undistorted.
- Static/plain background plates (scenes 3, 8, 12, 13, 14) are flagged as safe to build directly in the editing tool rather than spending generation budget on them — they're just ambient backdrops for text overlays.
