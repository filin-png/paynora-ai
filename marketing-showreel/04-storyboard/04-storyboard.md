# Stage 4 & 5 — Showreel Design + Second-by-Second Storyboard

## Concept

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, ~40 seconds.
**Style:** Premium tech commercial — dark, warm-neutral cinematic environment, glassmorphism panels with real product screenshots floating in 3D space, subtle reflections and light sweeps, minimal type, fast but readable pacing. No face, no cyberpunk neon, no hacker clichés.
**Read without sound:** every beat is carried by on-screen text + a distinct visual, so the ad works muted (per brief).
**Narrative:** HOOK → CAPABILITIES → PROOF #1 (Paynora) → PROOF #2 (Nexora) → TECH STACK → SERVICES → CTA. This follows the brief's suggested structure — it already matches what the two projects prove, so no restructuring was needed.

**Color grammar (deliberate, not decorative):**
- **Paynora block:** cool graphite/indigo dark UI (matches its actual product palette) — signals "financial precision, trust."
- **Nexora block:** warm-black + bronze + violet (matches its actual documented design system) — signals "AI presence, wealth intelligence." Violet specifically = "the AI did this," used only in AI-context scenes, mirroring the real product's own design rule.
- **Frame/CTA:** neutral dark with a single accent (bronze or indigo) — the developer's own identity, deliberately not borrowing either product's full palette.
